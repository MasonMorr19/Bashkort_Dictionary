## STATION VOCABULARY LISTS

Each station in the Ufa Memory Palace is loaded with thematically appropriate Bashkir vocabulary. Words are sourced from the Bashkir Swadesh list, Bashkir Wikipedia, cuisine articles, and the Ural-batyr epic. Bashkir uses Cyrillic script with 9 additional letters: Ә ә, Ө ө, Ү ү, Ғ ғ, Ҡ ҡ, Ң ң, Ҙ ҙ, Ҫ ҫ, Һ һ.

**Note:** Some vocabulary below is reconstructed from known Turkic cognates and linguistic patterns documented in academic sources. Words marked with ⚠️ should be verified against a native speaker or the Bashkir-Russian dictionary (Башҡортса-русса һүҙлек) before final use in the app. Unmarked words are sourced directly from Wikipedia's Bashkir language article, the Swadesh list, or Bashkir cuisine/cultural articles.
